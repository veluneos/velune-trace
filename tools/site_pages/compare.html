VeluneOS Compare

Compare a Reference run and a Target run of your robot — before and after a software, configuration or hardware change. Get a stated decision and the structural differences to review, each with its time window.

Request Beta access Sign in to Compare

What it reports

Structure, not payloads.

  • A stated decisionREVIEW_REQUIRED, NO_MATERIAL_STRUCTURAL_CHANGE, INCONCLUSIVE_DIFFERENCE or INSUFFICIENT_COVERAGE — never a blank.
  • Streams in one run onlyStreams present in the Reference run or the Target run, but not both.
  • Message interval changesRecorded intervals longer or shorter in Target, with values from both runs and whether they returned.
  • Activity loss and recoveryWhere a stream recorded no messages in one run, and when it came back.
  • Timestamp representationA change in timestamp precision, reported on its own rather than as a slowdown.
  • Evidence quality and coverageEach finding is comparable, limited or inconclusive; what could not be compared is listed.

Findings follow a fixed review order, not a severity score. Compare does not decide root cause, fault, safety, regression or release readiness — your engineers do.

Get the Adapter Kit.

The Beta Kit installs Velune Adapter and the velune command. Python 3.12 or 3.13. It runs on your machine and never uploads anything itself.
